# Slide 3

## Technical Approach

### Bullet Content

**Architecture**
- Five layers: data, ETL, AI, service, delivery
- Two corpora: incidents train, fatalities retrieve
- FastAPI service layer, 20+ endpoints
- Citation guardrail enforced before response

**AI Pipeline**
- SIF classifier, isotonic calibrated
- Life Saving Rule multi-label mapper
- Hazard NER plus 384-dim vector index
- Risk fusion with escalation override

**Methodology**
- Stratified 70/15/15, grouped by site
- Threshold tuned for recall, not accuracy
- Batch pre-scoring, pre-computed aggregates
- CPU-only inference, one-command deploy

**Risk Fusion Formula**
- 0.55·P(SIF) + 0.25·LSR + 0.10·barrier + 0.10·similarity

# Slide 4

## Feasibility & Viability

### Bullet Content

**Feasibility**
- Working prototype, 16,249 records scored
- One docker compose up, CPU-only
- Open-source stack, no licence cost
- 100,000 reports, no architecture change
- 34 automated smoke tests

**Challenges**
- Severe class imbalance in SIF events
- No public Life Saving Rule ground truth
- Barrier failure rarely stated in narratives
- Hallucination risk in generated CAPA
- CPU-only latency on field hardware

**Mitigation**
- Weighted loss, recall-first threshold tuning
- Weak supervision, reported as agreement
- Precision-first: missing means not stated
- Generation constrained to retrieved precedent
- Quantised inference, pre-computed aggregates
